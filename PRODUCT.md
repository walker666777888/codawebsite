# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
People who visit the website.

## Product Purpose
To serve as a premium, top-tier showcase website that amazes visitors with exceptional design, UI/UX, and smooth interactions.

## Positioning
An intensely crafted, visually stunning experience that stands out through its sheer quality, smoothness, and premium feel.

## Operating Context
Standard web browsing environment, focusing on modern browsers capable of rendering high-end animations, smooth scrolling, and advanced interactive experiences.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Technical Constraints: Must leverage the existing stack (Next.js, Tailwind v4, GSAP, Framer Motion, Three.js, Lenis) to achieve maximum smoothness and visual fidelity.
- Undecided: Specific content, branding assets, and detailed workflows are currently open.

## Evidence on Hand
An existing Next.js codebase with high-end animation and WebGL libraries installed and ready.

## Product Principles
- **Aesthetics First:** Prioritize a premium, top-tier visual experience that wows the user instantly.
- **Flawless Motion:** Ensure interactions and animations are incredibly smooth and performant.
- **Unconstrained Creativity:** Dream big and bold with distinct, outstanding craft since there are no rigid brand constraints.
